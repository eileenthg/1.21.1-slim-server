4 Oct 2026 comparison. Jars are in the session scratchpad and are not installed.

- **Installed YH:** `youkaishomecoming-4.2.14.jar` is yinyuze's unofficial port of the full 1.20.1 YH (CurseForge `youkais-homecoming-unofficial-port`). 4.2.15 is out (29 Sep). Its contents: 1,477 items, 1,116 blocks, 20 entities, 694 recipes.
- **Youkais' Feasts 1.1.0 (lcy0x1, id youkaisfeasts):** the food/cooking half. 290/291 items and 145/145 blocks are also in YH, as are the grapes/wine, sushi and tea. It reuses YH's Java package `dev.xkmc.youkaishomecoming` (154 identical classes), so it **can't be installed alongside YH**. It supports migrating objects from YH. It adds only udumbara_leaves and the "mellow" effect. It has no coffee, youkai flesh, mandrake or danmaku.
- **Gensokyo Legacy 1.0.1 (id gensokyolegacy):** characters, structures and RPG. It shares 14/17 entities and only 26/225 items with YH. It adds 59 structure pieces (versus YH's 3), quests/RPG, dolls, Rinnosuke and hexbrew. It requires TerraBlender, Patchouli and Curios. It has its own package, so it could coexist with YH but would duplicate the characters. It has only 2 danmaku items (danmaku_api is bundled), and Remilia is absent.
- **In neither mod:** 966 building blocks (concrete/sikkui/stairs/slab/vertical-slab, about 21% of the pack's blockstates), about 129 danmaku items, coffee, youkai flesh foods, mandrake, Remilia. It's unverified whether these were renamed or moved to other mods.

**Why:** the user is weighing YH (port) against the official split. See the earlier blockstate audit in modpack-audit/06-performance-and-paleoak.md.

**4 Oct follow-up:**
- Feasts' "udumbara_leaves" is the port's `udumbara_seeds` renamed. "mellow" is the port's `tea_polyphenols` renamed (field TEA). The Moon Lantern exists and works in port 4.2.15; only its tooltip lang lines are missing. Feasts has **no** new content compared with the port.
- YH building blocks (966) in structures: only 3 files use any of them, YH's own shrine/nests (sikkui, fine_grid_framed_shoji, a sikkui trapdoor) and TLM Spell's fairy_maid_cafe (8 sikkui/shoji variants). No structure uses the concrete or plain stairs/slabs.
- Gensouworks (gensouworks-…-ykhu-0.1.1, installed) furnishes 21 structures with about 140 YH food/furniture blocks in the `youkaishomecoming:` namespace. Swapping YH for Feasts would strip their interiors.
- Arcadian Dream: 84 items, 16 blocks, its own danmaku system, three generic fairies, hihiirokane/makaite gear and an abandoned shrine. It overlaps Legacy only thematically (danmaku, fairies, shrine, sake); no IDs collide.
- Legacy (LGPL-2.1) overlaps YH's 14 characters and the Gensouworks buildings (marisa_house vs kirisame_house, morichika_shop vs kourindou). Its unique content: multi-piece Hakurei Shrine, magical/sakura forest biomes, gap portal, Rinnosuke, hexbrew/alchemy, talismans, Alice dolls, umbrella, a big furniture/tile set. Its characters are its quest givers, so cutting them guts the mod; worldgen-level trimming by datapack is the realistic option.
- **Biome correction (4 Oct):** the YH port has 4 TerraBlender overworld biomes (sakura_forest, sakura_birch_forest, sakura_taiga, animal_path; region class compat/terrablender/GensokyoRegion), with **no biome lang names**, which is why Xaero shows raw IDs. Feasts has the same 4, named. Legacy's real biomes are magical_forest + sakura_forest (MagicalForestRegion, config `regionWeight` default 2; 0 disables both) plus `gap`, an empty dimension. Legacy's sakura_forest is a sparse duplicate (vanilla trees_cherry only, no spawners). Earlier "cirno_nest/hakurei_shrine/marisa_house" biome entries were biome tags, not biomes.
- **Lang fix (4 Oct):** now a code-free mod `mods/youkaiportlangfix-1.0.0.jar` (source `modpack-audit/youkai-port-langfix-mod/`, lowcodefml, boot-tested); the old resource pack `youkai-port-lang-fix.zip` was moved to `resourcepacks-out/retired/`. Originally (source `modpack-audit/resourcepacks-out/youkai-port-lang-fix/`, pack_format 34) adds 72 missing en_us keys to the YH port. 30 come from the port's own datagen classes (YHLangData enum, GLLang) and 42 from Feasts (biomes, block/item/fluid names, advancements, deer subtitles, progress text). Installed but **not enabled**; the user enables it in-game. It doesn't change any port key names (the port uses tooltip.flask_* / tooltip.steamer_*, not Feasts' flask.* / steamer.*).
- **Gensouworks 0.1.1:** structures only (1 class, no items/entities): eientei, koumakan (rare set, spacing 48), kirisame_house, margatroid_house, kourindou, larva_cave, signal_tower, sanyousei_new_house (common set, spacing 32). They use vanilla-biome tags; sanyousei_new_house also spawns in the YH sakura biomes. The loot tables draw on YH (88 entries), FD and TLM. There are no NPCs inside. Overlaps Legacy: kirisame_house vs marisa_house, kourindou vs morichika_shop.
- **Worldgen impact (4 Oct):** the overworld TerraBlender regions are vanilla 10 (config), Vanilla Backport 5, YUNG Cave Biomes 3 and YH sakura 2 (the last three hardcoded); total 20. Legacy would add magical_forest+sakura at 2 (config regionWeight), total 22. TB picks a region per cell with WeightedRandomLayer (random % totalWeight), so changing the total re-rolls about 69% of region cells in **newly generated** chunks only. Terrain shape doesn't change (regions only pick biomes); expect biome/vegetation seams at the edge of the generated area. Gensouworks has been installed since 1 Sep, before the pregen, so it changes nothing. An empirical before/after biome diff on a test-server world copy was offered, not done.
- **SUPERSEDED: the numbers in this bullet came from the broken reseeded predictor, see [[seedscout-seed-search]]. Measured 4 Oct (seed 463772255524938046, 1.79M samples at chunk spacing over the real world's box ±1024):** seedscout gained `/seedscout biomemap <seed> <x0> <z0> <x1> <z1> <step> <threads> <name>` (BiomeMap.java). It samples biome and climate without generating chunks. Driver: `X-expeditions/run-biomemap.sh <name>`. Outputs are in X-expeditions/seedscout/biomemap-{nogl,gl}*; the analysis scripts are in the session scratchpad (compare.py/bands.py/render2.py); the image is `modpack-audit/legacy-worldgen-impact.png`. Findings: terrain height is 100% identical; 13.1% of new land changes biome; magical forest takes 2.35% of new land and GL sakura 1.94%. Chunk edges along the generated border that would show a biome cut go from 11.0% to 21.2%. **GL's region ignores the climate gradient:** magical_forest sits 17% on ocean terrain and 6% frozen; GL sakura_forest sits 46% on ocean terrain and 13% frozen (over frozen/jagged peaks, grove). YH's sakura biomes stay strictly in forest/taiga climate.
- **Legacy and the YH port cannot coexist as shipped:** (1) GL's AT makes every Brain method public, which crashes Lithium's `mixin.ai.task.launch` overwrite (fix: lithium.properties `mixin.ai.task.launch=false`); (2) GL bundles danmaku_api 3.0.5+5, which removed `GrazeHelper$GrazeProvider` that the YH port needs, so YH crashes on load. It booted only with a test-only GL jar re-bundling 3.0.5+3 (`removed-mods/harness-extra-2026-10-04/`); untested beyond boot.
- Temperatures: Cold Sweat world.toml has no YH or GL biome entries. Its fallback uses base temp ± a downfall-based spread (magical 0.7 is roughly forest's 51–76F). seasonclimate classes biomes by base temperature, so the new biomes count as temperate. The user's idea: give magical_forest flower_forest's row `["…", 51, 76, "F"]`.
- **Option 2 built (4 Oct):** `modpack-audit/gensokyo-compat/` builds gensokyocompat-1.0.0.jar. It sets lithium:options `mixin.ai.task.launch=false` in mods.toml; MagicalForestRegionMixin sets weight 0 (no region re-roll) and replaces forest/dark_forest; GensokyoRegionMixin adds DARK_FOREST→magical_forest inside YH's region. Plus `danmaku_api-3.0.6-merged.jar` (+5 with +3's GrazeHelper* and PlayerHolder). Boot-tested with the original GL jar on X. NOT installed live. The user found the regen seams (tree layout differs; biomes identical) manageable and is leaning towards installing GL.
- **GL overlap facts:** the 26 duplicate items are all YH character items, plus Straw Hat (Eternal Tales) and Tatami Block (≈FD tatami). Furniture overlaps YH/TLM/Convenient Decor/Spectral Decorations/Quark. Structures: GL hakurei_shrine (biomes: cherry_grove + GL sakura, so with compat only cherry grove), marisa_house + morichika_shop (magical_forest only → rare with compat) overlap Gensouworks kirisame_house/kourindou and YH's shrine. Both YH and GL have their own Cirno frozen-frog GLMs and character_config systems (parallel duplicate characters). Effects native_god_bless/unconscious exist in both namespaces. GL adds l2tabs (inventory tab bar, new to the pack), the `gap` dimension, patchouli guide, curios head/back/charm tags, mixins on NaturalSpawner/ServerLevel (sealing), Raid, ChunkGenerator (multi_spread placement vs BSS), and the container-screen/GuiGraphics item rendering.
- **Corrected own-seed numbers (4 Oct):** X-expeditions/seed-tmp is an empty world with the live level.dat, used for exact maps (run-biomemap-own.sh). own-nogl matched the live surface biomes at 100.0% (4096/4096 in two areas). With GL + compat: magical forest takes 0.17% of samples, only where dark forest would be (temperate, never ocean); biome differs in 0.18% of not-yet-generated land; new biome seams are 56 border edges if all chunks are kept, 0 if never-visited chunks are regenerated. A real regen test (2238 chunks near spawn) gave identical biomes, but tree/feature placement differs inside regenerated chunks, and some structure starts were lost or gained. The harness extras were moved back to removed-mods/harness-extra-2026-10-04.
- **5 Oct Q&A findings:** Rumia's hut = YH youkai_nest (one visited at 312,88). YH Rumia attacks players with Youkaifying (from flesh food) or jerk/enemy reputation; **GL characters treat favor ≤0 as ENEMY** (hostile on sight by default). Native God Bless and Unconscious are identical code in both mods; YH also grants them from a drink, food and Koishi's attack. The live world has **0 YH Hakurei shrines** (13 cirno_nest, 13 youkai_nest; Gensouworks: 1 koumakan, 4 larva_cave, 1 margatroid_house, 3 signal_tower; none of kirisame/kourindou/eientei/sanyousei). YH shrine biomes: cherry_grove, flower_forest, meadow, #youkai_biomes, spacing 24/sep 8. GL multi_spread = 24 flat-check attempts per 32-chunk cell plus one-of-set picking; BSS hooks tryGenerateStructure, so it already covers GL. GL RaidMixin only reports a raid win to its quest system. Tabs: Legendary Tabs (+ Sophisticated Tab by otectus) vs L2Tabs (attributes/curios/GL golem); keep both and hide L2Tabs tabs. ModernFix `mixin.perf.dynamic_resources` is OFF (default) — a big memory win to try. Block-use scan (blockuse.py): ~104k of 135.7k block variants are placed by no structure/blueprint/worldgen.
