- **5 Oct 1.1.1 (INSTALLED 5 Oct 13:45; 1.1.0 archived in removed-mods/gensokyo-bridge-1.1.0-2026-10-05):** 1.1.0's Biolith `replaceOverworld` broke every world save. - **BSS facts (decompiled 1.4.0.2):** the whitelist skips spacing AND repetition bias but NOT the overlap check, and with countWhitelistedStructuresForSpacing=true whitelisted structures still count as neighbours. Spacing is size-scaled: required clearance = max(candidate, neighbour) base radius × 5^(normalized*2−1), normalized from footprint×√height (10..20000). The Legacy bounding boxes are big (score 16-27k), so shrines and shops need ~800 blocks clear of EVERY structure, houses ~430. BSS enabled for GL would leave ~4 of 94. Per-structure radius overrides can't soften it, since the neighbour's radius counts. Replay tool: scratchpad bsssim.py reads the index (validated: all conflicts among non-whitelisted pairs but 1 explained by first-occurrence forcing).
- **Removing a structure from generated chunks:** deleting its chunks isn't enough, because the same structure regenerates at the seed-derived spot. Prune its BSS index entry, otherwise BSS's overlap check rejects it against its own old record, then regenerate with a datapack that empties its structure_set. The datapack temporarily lands in level.dat DataPacks.Enabled, so restore that tag from a snapshot afterwards. Freed spots let other structures BSS had refused spawn there (8 appeared).
