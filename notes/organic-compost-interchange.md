8 Oct 2026: `organic-compost-interchange.zip` (source in proposed-datapacks/) installed in live `datapacks/`. Overrides the 3 FD/Corn Delight organic_compost recipes (same ids). Tags `modpack:compost_straw` (straw, dried_corn_husk, hay block, Quark+MCTradePost thatch, BWG cattail thatch) and `modpack:compost_corn_refuse` (corn_delight corncob, extradelight corn_cob, corn_husk, corn_silk). Not server-tested; colony-autoteach still teaches the fixed-item versions. See [[modpack-datapack-install-location]].

**Why:** user wanted straw and corncob slots interchangeable with equivalents.
**How to apply:** new compost-equivalent items go into the two tags.
